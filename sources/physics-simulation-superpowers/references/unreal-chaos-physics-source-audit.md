## Admission decisions

- Admit official conceptual boundaries and task-routing facts only.
- Pair CVD physics-state/query/solver evidence with Unreal Insights or documented timing evidence; neither is promoted as sufficient alone.
- Treat all exact APIs, nodes, commands, cvars, settings paths, plugins, and maturity claims as version-specific until verified in the declared patch/build.
- Community posts were not used as authority. They may only supply search terms for follow-up official verification.
- Bundled-evaluation-only: 60 Hz and server physics CPU p95 `<= 3 ms`; these are never runtime defaults or claims taken from Epic documentation. Runtime acceptance uses the active request's declared cadence and budget; undeclared values block acceptance.
