# Surveying real-time physics research: source audit

Read on 2026-08-30. Each official source or primary methodology source is claim-scoped: the Authority column says why it can support the Claim, while Scope and Limitation prevent discovery metadata, badges, or venue reputation from becoming stronger evidence than they are. Publication arrangements are year-specific. Crossref relations are depositor assertions. DOI comparison uses no Unicode normalization. A public repository with no license is not open source, and an artifact badge is not proof of every research or production claim.

| Official source | Authority | Scope | Claim | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [DOI Handbook](https://www.doi.org/doi-handbook/html/) | DOI Foundation policy | DOI syntax, URI/URN presentation, and comparison | Preserve the original DOI; parse recognized wrappers; percent-decode the representation exactly once as UTF-8; compare ASCII A-Z case-insensitively | Do not lowercase stored DOI text, decode repeatedly, or perform Unicode normalization |
| [Crossref REST API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) | Crossref service documentation | Deposited scholarly metadata retrieval | Log endpoint, filters, cursor, rows, and retrieval time | Metadata is depositor supplied and may be incomplete or corrected later |
| [Crossref versioning](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/principles-practices/best-practices/versioning/) | Crossref best practice | Versioned research objects | Retain distinct versions and their DOI identities | Versioning practice is not universal and does not establish work equivalence alone |
| [Crossref relationships](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/schema-library/markup-guide-metadata-segments/relationships/) | Crossref schema guide | Deposited relations such as preprint and version-of-record links | Store relation type, direction, source, and retrieval date | A relation is a depositor assertion, may be missing, and needs corroboration |
| [arXiv identifier](https://info.arxiv.org/help/arxiv_identifier.html) | arXiv identifier documentation | Modern and legacy arXiv IDs | Preserve exact versioned identifiers such as `arXiv:2401.00001v2` | An unversioned identifier does not pin a manifestation |
| [arXiv versions](https://info.arxiv.org/help/versions.html) | arXiv version policy | Revision history and availability | Prior versions remain identifiable while the abstract page defaults to the latest | Revision lineage does not prove equivalence to a publisher version |
| [PRISMA-S](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-search) | PRISMA reporting extension | Search-method reporting | Preserve sources, platforms, dates, full strategies, limits, and deduplication method | Reporting guidance does not select domain sources or prove search completeness |
| [PRISMA 2020 flow diagram](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-flow-diagram) | PRISMA statement | Identification, screening, inclusion, and exclusion counts | Publish auditable stage counts and exclusion reasons | A flow diagram does not validate the technical evidence in included papers |
| [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/) | ACM publication platform | Item-level ACM publication records and full text | Verify title, authors, DOI, outlet, and publication record at its ACM DL item page | The DL home page is an entry point; final evidence must cite the item-level record |
| [SIGGRAPH 2026 technical papers](https://s2026.siggraph.org/program/technical-papers/) | Official event program | SIGGRAPH 2026 tracks and publication route | Verify track, review path, and outlet for that exact year | Never infer TOG or conference-proceedings status from the SIGGRAPH name alone |
| [SCA](https://computeranimation.org/) | Official symposium site | Symposium year, calls, program, and publication links | Verify the exact year and whether the paper is in CGF, PACM CGIT, or another outlet | Publication arrangements change; a series label is not a timeless publisher rule |
| [I3D](https://i3dsymposium.org/) | Official symposium site | I3D calls, programs, and proceedings links | Verify archival track, review status, and outlet for the selected year | Talks, posters, and other tracks can have different evidence status |
| [High-Performance Graphics](https://www.highperformancegraphics.org/) | Official conference site | HPG calls, programs, and publication links | Verify whether an item belongs to an archival or non-archival track | A program appearance alone does not establish a peer-reviewed publication |
| [Eurographics Digital Library](https://diglib.eg.org/) | Eurographics Association repository | Eurographics and CGF records, PDFs, and proceedings | Use the item record to verify venue, year, title, authors, and persistent identifier | An index citation is discovery evidence until reconciled to this or another primary record |
| [IEEE RAS conferences](https://www.ieee-ras.org/conferences-workshops/) | IEEE Robotics and Automation Society | Sponsored robotics venues and official proceedings routes | Search the declared robotics venue years and reconcile publications to IEEE Xplore or the official proceedings | Sponsorship listings do not establish that every program item is archival or relevant |
| [RSS proceedings](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/) | Official Robotics: Science and Systems archive | Yearly RSS proceedings and stable article records | Search eligible years directly and retain the official proceedings URL when a DOI is absent | Older records can lack DOIs and still require identity reconciliation |
| [OpenAlex API recipes](https://help.openalex.org/how-to/api-recipes/) | OpenAlex service documentation | Discovery queries, filters, pagination, and work graph | Log exact API query and cursor; use results for candidate and citation discovery | OpenAlex is a discovery index, not final bibliographic authority |
| [GitHub license API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/licenses/licenses) | GitHub API documentation | Detected repository license metadata | Record detected license and retrieval revision or date | Detection can miss custom licenses and says nothing complete about dependencies, data, models, or assets; no license grants no reuse permission |
| [GitHub permanent links](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/working-with-files/using-files/getting-permanent-links-to-files) | GitHub repository documentation | Commit-pinned file and line links | Cite immutable commit URLs for inspected source evidence | A branch URL can drift and a permanent link does not establish authorship or correctness |
| [Zenodo version management](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/manage-versions/) | Zenodo repository documentation | Concept and version records for deposits | Store the version DOI for the exact archived artifact release | A concept DOI identifies the family rather than one reproducible artifact version |
| [ACM artifact review and badging](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-badging) | ACM publication policy | Defined artifact availability, functionality, and result badges | Record the exact badge and its stated review scope | A badge is not proof of all paper claims, portability, maintenance, or production readiness |
| [Graphics Replicability Stamp](https://www.replicabilitystamp.org/requirements.html) | Stamp program requirements | Graphics artifact availability and replicability review | Record the tested artifact, platform, and stamp scope | The stamp is not proof of general performance, correctness, or game integration readiness |
| [Wohlin snowballing method](https://doi.org/10.1145/2601248.2601268) | Primary peer-reviewed methodology paper | Backward and forward snowballing in systematic studies | Declare seeds, directions, rounds, screening, and stop rule | The method does not define a universal saturation threshold or replace database searches |

## Audit policy

The table is the pinned claim map for this skill version. During a live survey, save retrieval timestamps, exact queries, response checksums, and relevant source snapshots where licensing permits. If an upstream page changes, preserve the prior observation, mark the drift, and re-evaluate only the claims whose authority or limitation changed.
